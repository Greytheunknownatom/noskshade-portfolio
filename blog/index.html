The journal

What I’m learning while designing products, brands and systems from Abuja, written the way I’d explain it to a friend.

UI/UX & Product

I kept forgetting to drink water in the heat, so I designed and built HeatBud

With El Niño in the news and my days packed with work, I kept noticing the heat only after it had already got to me. So I made a small app to warn me first, and it’s live today.

UI/UX & Product

AI can design a screen in ten seconds. That’s exactly why you still need a designer.

Last month a founder showed me 40 screens he’d generated over a weekend. They looked great. None of them answered the one question his users were asking.

UI/UX & Product

Nobody told me the hardest part of designing a savings app would be fear

I thought I was designing for people who wanted to save money. I was really designing for people who had already lost it.

Design systems

I built my first design system backwards. Here’s what I’d do instead.

I started with buttons and cards. Three weeks later I had three versions of every button and a developer who had stopped asking me questions.

Design x code

I’m a designer learning to code. Week one humbled me in a way I didn’t expect.

I thought the hard part would be the syntax. It was realising how many of my designs were missing half their screens.

Brand identity

The hardest part of refreshing a school’s brand was deciding what not to change

Everyone had an opinion. Alumni loved the old look, parents wanted clarity, students just wanted the portal to work.

UI/UX & Product

Five people broke my onboarding in one afternoon. Best afternoon of the project.

I was proud of those eight setup questions. By question four, every single tester was tapping skip without reading.

Brand identity

“I just need a logo.” Why I usually say no to that first.

Most small businesses don’t lose customers because of their logo. They lose them because nothing they post looks like it comes from the same place.

Design systems

The day I realised my developer didn’t understand my Figma file

“Is Card/Large the same as Tile/Primary?” It was. I just never told anyone, and the names didn’t either.

Design x code

My app had five screens in Figma and twenty on a real phone

The first time I shipped something myself, the internet in Abuja taught me more about design than any tutorial.
