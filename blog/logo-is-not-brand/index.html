“I just need a logo.” Why I usually say no to that first.

Most small businesses don’t lose customers because of their logo. They lose them because nothing they post looks like it comes from the same place.

Philip Nosike · 30 September 2026 · 4 min read

Nearly every new brand enquiry I get starts the same way.

“I need a logo.”

After years of freelance work, I now reply with a question: where will people actually see your business this month?

The answer is usually a WhatsApp status, a few Instagram posts, a flyer and maybe a shop sign.

The logo isn’t what makes those work

It helps. But what makes people remember you is seeing the same colours, the same tone and the same feel everywhere they meet you.

What I suggest starting with instead

  • One sentence that says what you do and who it’s for.
  • Two brand colours and one neutral. Used every single time.
  • One font for headings, one for everything else.
  • Three ready-to-use templates: a post, a status and a flyer.
  • Then the logo, designed to sit comfortably inside all of that.
Consistency beats cleverness. A simple mark used everywhere does more than a brilliant one used once.

If you’re a small business owner reading this, look at your last five posts side by side. Do they look like they came from the same business?
