Case Study · Higher Education · 2026

HillCross College

A homepage hero revamp for the January 2027/2028 admissions intake, designed to tell prospective students that applications are open and move them straight into an application.

Role
Product Designer
Scope
Hero, announcement bar, nav
Platform
Web, responsive
Goal
Applications for the new intake
hillcross.ac.za
01 · The brief

An intake campaign needed a front door

Each year HillCross opens applications for a new intake. The homepage hero was the first thing prospective students saw, but it was written as a general welcome: a long institutional paragraph, one apply button and a cut-out stock photo. Nothing on the page said that admissions were open, or for which year.

The brief was to rebuild the hero around the January 2027/2028 intake and keep the credibility markers the college already had.

02 · Before and after

From a welcome to an invitation

Before

General welcome headline, a 63-word institutional paragraph, one CTA and the stats tucked bottom right.

After

Intake announcement bar, intake-led headline, two calls to action, campus photography and slider controls.

03 · What changed

Five decisions behind the new hero

01

An announcement bar for the intake

A full-width red bar states the intake in plain words and carries its own Apply Now button. It sits above the navigation on every page, so a student who lands deep in the site still sees that admissions are open.

02

A headline about the student

"Welcome to Hillcross College" became "Your Next Chapter Starts this Intake." The orange highlight on Intake ties the headline to the campaign, and the 63-word institutional paragraph became one line naming the academic year.

03

Two clear next steps

"Start your application" is the primary action. "Entry requirements" sits beside it as the secondary path for students who want to check eligibility before committing.

04

Proof at the moment of decision

The 3000+ graduates, 90% employment rate and 24+ departments moved from the bottom right into a glass panel under the calls to action, where they support the decision to apply.

05

Real campus photography

The cut-out stock group was replaced with students photographed on campus. A dark scrim on the left keeps the white headline readable over the photo.

04 · Why a slider

Why the hero became a slider

One campaign, more than one story
The intake message leads on the first slide. Later slides can carry faculties, campus life or open days without crowding a single frame.
Swappable each season
When the intake closes, only the campaign slide changes. The rest of the hero stays, so there is no redesign every January.
The visitor stays in control
Previous and next buttons, a 02 / 02 counter, a progress bar and a pause button mean the slider never moves content away from someone who is still reading.
Part of the campaign palette
The yellow progress bar and white controls pick up the announcement bar colours, so the slider reads as part of the admissions campaign.
