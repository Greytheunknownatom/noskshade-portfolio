HillCross College Student Dashboard

A revamp of the legacy student platform into a dashboard that walks a new student from registration to their first module, and keeps returning students one click from class.

The result

One dashboard that always shows a student their next step, from Start Registration to their first module, with support on every view.

What I did

I redesigned the legacy student platform: the registration card and its three states, the e-portal assessment flow, and the components shared with the teacher and admin portals.

The problem

New students saw every tool at once and didn't know what to do first.

New students didn’t know what to do first

The old platform showed every tool to every student, whether they had registered or not. Registration status, onboarding and the eLearning portal were hard to find, and support was a separate journey.

The goal: one dashboard that always shows the student’s next step, from starting registration to opening their first lesson.

Registration status that changes with the student

Start registration

Pending Registration with Start Registration as the only action. The Virtual Classroom, onboarding video and Notice Board are already visible, so a new student can explore before they enrol.

Introductory assessments unlock the first module

A banner explains that the initial tests unlock the first module lessons. Upcoming Deadlines stays empty, with one action: Start Test.

The Aptitude Test turns green, and the first tasks appear as Upcoming Deadlines with a Begin button for each.

What college students told us

Students used the revamped dashboard and shared what helped and what got in the way.

“The old portal showed me tools I couldn’t use yet. Now it just tells me the next step.”
“Support and my courses are on the same page, so I stopped emailing to ask where things are.”
“The checklist made the first week feel manageable.”

Shared with the teacher and admin portals

A persistent sidebar

Dashboard, Online Application, E-Portal, Timetable, Result Checker and Account Statement, always in the same place.

Status cards

One red status card with a phase label, a progress underline and a single primary action that changes per state.

Media cards

Dark photo cards for onboarding video and the Orientation Tutorial, with watch time and one play action.

Support everywhere

Notice Board alerts and a Live Chat Support bar at the bottom of every dashboard view.

The trade-off

One primary action per state means fewer shortcuts on screen. In return, a new student never has to guess what comes next.

What's next

Measure the time from sign-up to first lesson, and how many support messages still ask where things are.
