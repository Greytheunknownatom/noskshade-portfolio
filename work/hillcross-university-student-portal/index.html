HillCross University Student Portal

A web platform that takes students from their first application to everyday study, with an AI assistant that plans around how they learn.

The result

13 student flows designed end to end, from first application to exam day, on one design system that the lecturer and admin portals will share.

What I did

I designed the student portal and the system behind all three portals: applications with a live APS score and ID checks, an adaptive dashboard, AI study planning, proctored exams and finance.

The problem

Students needed one place to apply, register, study and pay, instead of paperwork and queues.

Three portals for one university

The platform is made of three connected web portals: one for students, one for lecturers and one for administrators. This case study covers the student portal, from creating an account and applying, through registration, courses, exams, grades and finance.

Student portal

Application, dashboard, courses, calendar, grades, finance, library, mentorship, internships, documents and support.

Lecturer portal

Where lecturers run their courses: lessons, live classes, assignments, exams and grading.

Admin portal

Where university staff manage admissions, registration, finance and student records.

From first application to exam day

Three users, one university

HillCross needed connected portals for students, lecturers and administrators. The student portal came first, and its design system set the rules the other two would follow.

Applying without the paperwork

A new student creates an account in three steps. Their nationality sets the fee plan, their ID is checked against Home Affairs, and the portal calculates a preliminary APS score as results are entered.

Always knowing where you stand

After submitting, the student sees the auto-check results, an assigned reviewer and a live timeline. The decision arrives as an admission offer with an accept-by date and the Year 1 fee breakdown.

A dashboard that changes with the student

Before admission, academic modules stay locked. During a break, friendly empty states take over. Once the semester starts, the dashboard fills with study focus, graded work and the week ahead.

An assistant that plans the day

AI Assist reads past study sessions and suggests what to do first, with time and confidence estimates. On assignments it shows the rubric and offers a draft review.

Fair exams from anywhere

Before a proctored exam, the lobby checks camera, microphone and presence. During the exam, a timer, invigilator video, question navigator and autosave keep the student focused.

Launching soon

The student portal is designed end to end and preparing for launch. The lecturer and admin portals follow on the same design system.

Pick a flow, follow every screen

Sign In & Registration

How a student gets into the portal: creating an account in three steps, signing in as a returning student, and recovering a forgotten password.

New students start here. Step 1 collects nationality, SA ID or passport number, phone number and email address. Proceed stays inactive until the form is complete, with Google and Apple as sign-up alternatives.

What students told us while testing

Applicants, first-year and returning students tried the portal and talked us through it.

“Seeing my APS score while I typed my results told me which courses I could apply for before I wasted time.”
“The study plan said what to do first today. I usually just open everything and panic.”
“Uploading proof of payment and seeing it marked Pending means I don’t have to queue at the finance office.”

Building the system while designing the product

With the student portal growing module by module and two more portals to follow, the design system was built alongside the screens and maintained as the project grew. Every new screen either used an existing component or added one to the library.

Foundations first

HillCross red, neutral greys, one type scale and a spacing grid were set before the first module, so every screen started from the same base.

Components from real screens

The sidebar, metric cards, course cards, status chips, tabs, side panels and modals were built as they were needed and reused across all 13 modules.

States as part of the system

Empty, locked, in progress, pending review and success states were designed once and applied everywhere, from semester breaks to exams that have not opened yet.

Maintained across three portals

Changes are made in the library and flow into the student, lecturer and admin portals, so the three products stay consistent as each one grows.

One guide behind three portals

The HillCross guide serves the student, lecturer and admin portals. It was built alongside the student portal and maintained as the other two were added.

The trade-off

AI Assist suggests a task order with time and confidence estimates instead of setting the plan for the student. It's less automatic, but the student stays in charge of their day.
