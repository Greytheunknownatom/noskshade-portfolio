KOYNX Admin

The operations side of KOYNX: one portal where staff verify members, watch every savings product and money flow, resolve risk, and control who can do what.

The result

One portal for every operations job: KYC in three tiers, risk ranked by severity, and role-based access, with every action logged by IP and timestamp.

What I did

I designed the portal end to end on the KOYNX design system: sign-in, overview, users, savings, groups, transactions, KYC, support and risk, and sub-admins.

The problem

Staff had to verify members, watch every money flow and resolve risk without ever approving someone by accident.

Four jobs the portal does every day

Pending actions first

The overview opens on what needs a decision now: KYC reviews, flagged accounts and the withdrawal queue, above users, deposits, payouts and system health.

KYC in three tiers

Tier 1 gives basic access with a R30,000 daily limit. Tier 2 checks name and ID, and Tier 3 adds proof of address. Accept stays disabled until every document is verified.

Risk with severity and status

Fraud flags and risk alerts are ranked by severity and tracked from open to resolved, with actions to view, close or escalate.

Roles and permissions

Each sub-admin gets one role and any number of permissions inside it. Every action is logged with IP and timestamp.

Admin portal sign in

Work email and password, an option to stay signed in for 24 hours, and a support contact for staff without credentials.

What the operations team told us

Staff who verify members and handle risk tested the admin portal on real tasks.

“Accept staying disabled until every document is checked stops me approving someone by accident.”
“Sorting tickets by severity means fraud flags never sit behind a password reset.”
“One role per admin with permissions inside it is much easier to audit than custom access for everyone.”

One guide for mobile, web and admin

The KOYNX style guide was written during the rebrand from Hillcross Pay, so the new identity and the product UI were defined together and shared by every surface.

The trade-off

Accept stays disabled until every KYC document is verified. Each review takes an extra step, but nobody gets approved by mistake.

What's next

The portal launches with KOYNX in South Africa. Feedback from reviewers and risk staff goes back into the shared components, so the member app and the admin portal stay in step.
