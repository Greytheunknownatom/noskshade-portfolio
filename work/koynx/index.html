Fintech · Mobile, web & admin

KOYNX

Save Smart, Withdraw Wise. Personal savings, Smart Esusu and Stokvel in one app, built on the trust South Africans already save with.

The result

Three surfaces (mobile, web and admin) designed end to end on one design system. Every money-moving flow passed compliance review with zero redesign cycles, and new screens now start 70% done.

What I did

I led the design. I took Hillcross Pay through its rebrand to KOYNX, set up the design system, and designed the member app, the web app and the admin portal.

The problem

South Africans save through banking apps, Esusu pots and stokvels kept in notebooks and on WhatsApp, and nothing shows them every rand in one place.

How a college payment app became a savings platform

A product built for one college

KOYNX began as Hillcross Pay, a financial services product designed in 2025. In January 2026 the brief changed: rebrand it and take it to a broader South African market.

People saving alone and together

Some save towards their own goals. Others pay into a rotating Esusu pot or a stokvel through cash, notebooks and WhatsApp. All of them needed to see every rand and trust where it goes.

A new name and three ways to save

The rebrand brought a new identity and split saving into Personal, Smart Esusu and Stokvel, each with its own colour and flow.

Every rand on record

Deposits, contributions and payouts are logged in one list, so members can always see where their money went.

From the problem to three design decisions

What we learned about how people save, the rules that came out of it, and the decisions those rules led to.

Savings built on trust, cash and paper

Personal goals live in banking apps, Esusu pots and stokvels in notebooks and WhatsApp. No single place shows a saver everything.

People don't save alone. They save in front of their group.

Interviews with personal savers, Esusu members and stokvel treasurers pointed the same way: visibility is the feature, whether it is your own goal or your group's pot.

Four rules every screen had to follow

Set before the first wireframe, checked on every flow.

The group leads, the individual follows

The group pot comes first, the personal balance one level down.

Withdrawals are deliberate, not punished

A calm, stepped flow that restates the group rules, then pays out without delay.

One design system, three surfaces

Shared tokens and components across mobile, web and admin. New screens now start at 70% done.

What savers told us

People who save on their own and in groups tried personal savings, Smart Esusu and Stokvel, and explained how they save today.

“Seeing how close I am to my target, with Top up right next to it, made me add money there and then.”
“The payout order is the first thing I check. If I can see when my turn comes, I trust the group.”
“I keep our contributions in a notebook and argue about it on WhatsApp. One list everyone can see would end that.”

Three surfaces, one system

The story is told on mobile, where members save. The web app and the admin portal follow as separate surfaces built on the same system.

Dashboard before verification

A new member lands on a dashboard that shows what is still locked and what to do first.

Signing up on the web

Step 1 of 3 asks for a phone number and sends a one-time code. The left panel rotates through the product promise, starting with bank-level security.

The system behind every rand

Staff verify KYC tiers, watch savings and group pots, resolve fraud and risk tickets, and manage sub-admins with role-based permissions. It is large enough to need its own case study.

Built once, kept in step across all three

One set of tokens

KOYNX red, the neutrals, status colours and type scale are shared by the mobile app, the web app and the admin portal.

Shared components

Stat cards, data tables, filters, status pills, tier badges, modals and success states are built once and reused on every surface.

Product colours that carry over

Personal savings, Smart Esusu and Stokvel keep the same colours from the member app into the admin dashboards, so staff and members read them the same way.

Maintained during the build

New screens were assembled from the library first. When a pattern changed, such as the KYC tiers or log tabs, the component was updated and every screen using it followed.

Ready for launch

Surfaces designed end to end across mobile, web and admin on one design system.

Preparing for public launch in South Africa.

I would test the withdrawal cooling step earlier. It carried the biggest trust risk and we validated it late.

Pilot feedback is feeding directly into the system, and once bank agreements close, the same three surfaces go live without a redesign.

One guide for mobile, web and admin

The KOYNX style guide was written during the rebrand from Hillcross Pay, so the new identity and the product UI were defined together and shared by every surface.
