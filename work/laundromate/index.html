On-demand · Mobile app

Laundromate

Laundry collected from your door and delivered back clean, booked in one flow from price check to receipt, for customers across Africa.

The result

A complete booking app with 8 flows. Prices show in local currency before sign-up, and the receipt matches the review screen line for line.

What I did

I designed the app end to end: onboarding, location-based prices, services that stack in one order, scheduled pickup and delivery, checkout and receipt.

The problem

Laundry services hide prices until you call, and pickup times are hard to pin down.

Laundry eats the weekend

Washing, ironing and getting bulky items like duvets cleaned takes hours many busy people don’t have. Laundry services exist, but prices are unclear until you call, and pickup times are hard to pin down.

Who it’s for: busy professionals, families and students in cities across Africa, from Nairobi in Kenya to Abuja in Nigeria, who would rather book a pickup than spend a day on laundry.

What shaped the design

Price is the first question

People want to know the cost before they commit, in their own currency and for their own city.

Timing has to fit around work

Collection and delivery need their own day and time window, and regular customers want a repeat schedule.

Orders are mixed

One bag often holds washing, ironing and a duvet, so services must stack in a single order.

No surprises at checkout

Every fee, offer and tip should be visible before paying, and the receipt should match it.

From an empty basket to a full order

Four services with prices and what’s included. Nothing selected, Next waiting.

Each added service fills with its colour, gains a counter, and the button reads (4) Next.

Five choices that shape the booking

Prices before an account

Visitors pick a country and city and see prices before signing up, in the local currency: Kenyan shillings (Ksh) in Kenya, naira (NGN) in Nigeria.

Services that stack

Each service has its own colour. Added cards fill with it, gain an item counter, and the button counts the order from (1) Next to (4) Next.

Wash preferences up front

A bottom sheet asks for mixed or separate wash and an optional high-temperature wash, with the price on each option.

Pickup on the customer's schedule

Collection and delivery each get a day, time window and handover method, with special instructions and a repeat frequency.

Every charge on one screen

Review order shows services, address, timings and each fee. The offer applies in place, the driver tip is optional, and the receipt mirrors the layout.

What laundry customers told us

Customers booked a pickup end to end and told us where they hesitated.

“Seeing prices before signing up is the reason I didn’t close the app.”
“Picking a time window for both pickup and drop-off fits around school runs.”
“When the bonus made delivery free, I could see the total change before paying.”

One colour per service, one pattern per state

Bottom sheets for preferences, so the service list stays in view.

One loading pattern, the washing-machine mark, reused for splash, saving details and adding a card.

Edit links on every section of the review screen, mirrored on the receipt.

From sign-up to receipt

Welcome

The first screen: a real customer moment, the Laundromate name and "Africa’s leading 24/7 Laundry app", with Lets begin, Log in, and Google or Apple sign-in.

What shipped

A complete booking app: account and password recovery, location-based prices, service selection with preferences, scheduled pickup and delivery, and a checkout whose receipt matches the review screen line for line.

Clean blue with a warm accent

Laundromate’s guide uses a fresh blue for trust and a coral accent for actions, with one colour per service so orders read at a glance.

The trade-off

Showing prices before an account means some visitors leave without signing up. Testers said it was the reason they stayed.

What's next

Expand to more cities. New markets only need currency and city data, not new components.
