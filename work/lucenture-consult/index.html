Brand & web · Consultancy

Lucenture Consult

A website for an Enugu business consultancy that helps startups and growing businesses with strategy, operations, marketing, technology and analytics.

The result

A live four-page site with a booking flow, where Book a call is always within reach and every service says who it's for.

What I did

I designed the brand and wordmark, the visual language and the website: home, services, case studies, about, booking and contact.

The problem

Small business owners couldn't tell which consulting service fit them without calling first.

Calm, premium and easy to trust

A widely spaced, all-caps wordmark. The E is drawn as three gold bars, a quiet nod to structure and stacked priorities, and the only colour in the mark. The line Strategy. Insight. Impact. sits underneath between two hairlines.

It works in white on the dark hero and on photography, and stays readable at the small size used in the navigation bar.

A bold serif for headlines, with one key word picked out in gold. A clean sans serif for navigation, body copy and buttons.

Real teams, warm light. Photography of people working together, darkened behind the hero so the message leads.

Glass and pills. A floating glass navigation bar, a glass hero card and pill buttons with gold outlines.

Quiet light sections. Off-white pages with thin dividers, numbered gold lists and generous spacing.

Four pages and a booking flow

Mission, value pillars, services, process, a featured case study, testimonials and FAQs.

Three core consulting services and two additional ones.

Client stories that open in a modal with summary and outcome.

The mission and the founding story.

A booking modal linked to the team calendar, with what happens next.

A short form, plus email, address and office hours.

How the work is explained to clients

Discovery

A relaxed first call about the business, its goals and what feels heaviest.

Diagnosis

A look at how things work today, naming the constraint holding growth back.

Strategy

A plan with priorities, owners and a timeline that fits the team.

Implementation Support

Staying close while the plan takes hold, adjusting along the way.

The trade-off

Dark team photography in the hero sets a premium tone but competes with the text. I darkened the photos so the message leads.

What's next

Measure booked calls from each page, and refine the services copy around the questions people ask on those calls.
