WeMove Fitness

A fitness app tailored to each user, where workouts, steps and closed rings earn collectible move mints.

The result

A seven-step onboarding that ends in the first reward, and a collectible mint system that testers said gave them a reason not to skip a day.

What I did

As UI/UX designer, I designed onboarding, home and profile, workouts, Quick Burst and rewards, plus the visual language and style guide.

The problem

People needed a reason to come back tomorrow, not just a log of what they did today.

Give people a reason to come back tomorrow

WeMove turns daily activity into progress you can see and keep. Onboarding builds a profile from a few taps, the home screen tracks calories, steps, weight and food, and every workout, challenge and closed ring unlocks a move mint.

The goal: a fitness app that feels personal from the first screen and rewarding every time it is opened.

From first tap to first mint

Tell us about yourself

A welcome by name, then the first of seven steps: male or female. A progress bar of dashes runs toward a trophy at the top.

Four choices that keep people moving

One question per screen

Seven short steps with big tap targets and ranges instead of typing. The dashed bar shows how close the trophy is.

A reward before the first workout

Finishing onboarding earns the first move mint, so the reward system is understood from day one.

Timers that pay out

Quick Burst shows the token for each finishing time up front, so a run becomes a challenge with a clear prize.

Mints worth collecting

Mints are African-inspired coin artworks in classic, epic and legendary tiers, shown locked until they are earned.

What fitness users told us

Active and casual users tried workouts, rings and move mints.

“Closing a ring with just a walk made me feel like it counted.”
“Earning something I can keep for a streak gives me a reason not to skip.”
“It asked how active I am first, so the goals didn’t feel impossible.”

Dots, dashes and two bold colours

Cards carry a dotted and dashed texture, like a running track seen from above. Deep teal marks progress, yellow marks the next action, and charcoal cards hold timers and challenges.

Energy yellow on deep teal

WeMove’s guide uses a high-energy yellow over deep teal, with reward artwork that follows African-inspired motifs.

The trade-off

Giving a mint before the first workout spends a reward early. It also teaches the reward system on day one, which mattered more.

What's next

I'd test whether streak rewards still bring casual walkers back after the first month, not just the first week.
